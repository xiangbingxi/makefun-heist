THE VELVET VAULT

One thief. Three rooms. One night to take it all.

How to play

  • WASD / arrows — move the thief. Shift — sneak (slower, harder to spot).
  • E (hold) — crack the big target, or hack the camera panel to kill the cameras for 12 seconds.
  • Q or click — toss a coin where the mouse points; the nearest guard goes to look at it.
  • F — drop a smoke pellet at your feet. No eye or camera sees through smoke.
  • Red cones are guard and camera sight. Blue floor is shadow — hide in it. Gold is loot.
  • Take the big target, then reach the green exit. If the alarm meter fills, you are caught.
  • P pause · M sound · R retry the room · Enter start / next room

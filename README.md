One thief. Three rooms. One night to take it all.

A top-down 2D stealth game that runs offline in a desktop browser from its
own static files. Sneak through a midnight museum — the Gallery, the Vault
Hall and the Penthouse — past patrolling guards with vision cones and
sweeping cameras. Grab the small loot, crack the big target in each room,
and slip out the exit before the alarm fills.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrows** — move. **Shift** — sneak (slower, harder to spot).
- **E (hold)** — crack the big target, or hack a camera terminal to kill
  the cameras for 12 seconds.
- **Q or click** — toss a coin where the mouse points; the nearest guard
  goes to look at it.
- **F** — drop a smoke pellet at your feet. No eye or camera sees through
  smoke.
- Red cones are guard and camera sight. Blue floor is shadow — hide in it.
  Gold is loot.
- Take the big target, then reach the green exit. If the alarm meter
  fills, you are caught and the room restarts.
- **P** pause · **M** sound · **R** retry the room · **Enter** start /
  next room

## Run it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html`, or run
`node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080/.
